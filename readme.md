# 🩺 HealthSeers

### See the Risk. Stop the Outbreak.

**HealthSeers** is an AI-powered community health monitoring and early warning system designed to help detect potential **water-borne disease outbreaks** before they escalate.

Developed for **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026**.

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH25001  
**Team:** HealthSeers

---

## 🌊 Problem Statement

### Smart Community Health Monitoring and Early Warning System for Water-Borne Diseases in Rural Northeast India

Water-borne disease outbreaks can spread rapidly in vulnerable communities. By the time a significant increase in reported cases becomes visible, many people may already have been exposed.

Health, environmental, and disease-related signals are often fragmented and difficult to analyze together.

HealthSeers aims to shift the approach from:

> **Reactive Outbreak Response**

to:

> **Proactive Outbreak Prevention**
